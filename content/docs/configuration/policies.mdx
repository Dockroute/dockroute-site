`DOCKROUTE_POLICY` bounds what a reconcile is allowed to do, with the same
semantics as ExternalDNS:

| Policy           | Creates | Updates owned | Deletes owned orphans |
| ---------------- | ------- | ------------- | --------------------- |
| `sync` (default) | ✓       | ✓             | ✓                     |
| `upsert-only`    | ✓       | ✓             | —                     |
| `create-only`    | ✓       | —             | —                     |

Two rules hold under **every** policy:

- Records DockRoute does not [own](/docs/concepts/safety-model) are never
  touched — conflicts are logged and skipped.
- Policies only *restrict*; they never grant extra behavior.

## Choosing a policy

- **`sync`** — the intended mode. Containers appear, records appear;
  containers go away, their records go away. Use it once you trust your
  labels.
- **`upsert-only`** — records are created and kept current, but nothing is
  ever deleted. Good while migrating an existing zone, or when stopped
  containers should keep resolving.
- **`create-only`** — records are created once and then left alone. Useful
  when another system takes over records after creation.

## Orphan cleanup

An *orphan* is a record whose container is gone but whose ownership TXT proves
it belongs to this instance. Under `sync`, orphans (and their TXTs) are removed
on the next reconcile. A dangling ownership TXT whose data record was deleted
by hand is also cleaned up under `sync` — or the record is recreated if its
container is still running.
